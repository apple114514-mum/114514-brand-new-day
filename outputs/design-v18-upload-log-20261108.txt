[E] closure 3: heat accounting is one multiplicative factor
==============================================================================
momentum + energy, steady natural circulation:
  K u^2/2 = g beta H dT ,  Q = rho u A cp dT
  => u^3 = 2 g beta H Q /(K rho cp A)
ratios, with u_R^ideal = sqrt(L_R):
  (u_R/u_R^ideal)^3 = PHI / K_R ,   D = (PHI/K_R)^(1/3) - 1
  PHI = [H_R Q_R/(A_R L_R^1.5)] * [beta_R/(rho_R cp_R)]
PHI=1 requires Q_R = A_R L_R^1.5/H_R = 0.268316 (= m_R:
  dT_R=1 and unit property ratios). So the heater must FOLLOW THE FLOW:
  Q_M = 0.2683 Q_P(q_m) at every point. Constant power breaks PHI.
leverage dD/dlnPHI = 1/3:
  1% error in Q_R (or dT_R, or cp_R) -> 0.33 pp of D
  3% on D from heat alone -> PHI budget 9.27%
property ratio, model run 100 K colder (473 vs 573 K, NEA 2015):
  beta 0.98763  rho 1.01252  cp 1.01368  -> PHI 0.96225 (-3.78%)
  -> D shift -1.27 pp, i.e. 42% of the 3% budget.
