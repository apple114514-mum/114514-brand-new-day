[0] baseline and vocabulary
  core_v2.py sha256[:16] = f76f81934141869a
  copy inside the uploaded zip: f76f81934141869a  (identical=True)
  Phi(m; x, Q) = dp_buoy - dp_fric.  The accepted flow is its root.

  Three kinds of number appear in this log and they are kept apart:
    searched   a value found by evaluating the model at many points.
               It is a lower bound on the range and nothing more.
    analytic   a range valid under stated premises, evaluated in
               double precision at its endpoints, no rounding proof.
    certified  an interval produced by outward-rounded arithmetic on a
               proven enclosure, valid for every point of the stated
               set.  Only this column may be called a bound.
  Everything labelled certified below comes from ivcore.py: every
  arithmetic result is widened by math.nextafter at each operation and
  every transcendental enters only through a proven monotone image.

  Reading: on every slab, dPhi/deps_M, dPhi/dk_aux_M, dPhi/du_loss_fac,
  dPhi/dmu_fac, dPhi/dcp_fac and dPhi/dL_HS_M are strictly negative,
  dPhi/drho_fac and dPhi/dd_hole strictly positive, dPhi/dQ strictly
  positive.  Only the inlet-control axis changes sign: raising the
  inlet temperature raises buoyancy and raises viscosity, and over this
  box the two nearly cancel.  It is therefore not pinned to a corner;
  its effect enters through the mean-value form in bound.py, whose
  enclosure is about 5 Pa against 510 Pa for plain interval evaluation
  of the same 4 K span; that factor is measured in section [3].

[4] uniqueness: dPhi/dm < 0 on a finite cover of each bracket
  Covering, not sampling: the bracket is cut into slabs and each slab
  is checked as an interval over the whole box and the whole power
  slab.  A finite cover plus continuity gives strict monotonicity on
  the entire bracket; a grid of points would not.

  loop  slabs  worst upper bound   narrowest |dPhi/dm|   verdict
  P        24            -5915.99              5915.99      strictly decreasing
  M        24            -17122.1              17122.1      strictly decreasing

  dPhi/dm stays below -5900 Pa per kg/s everywhere on the bracket, so
  the root is simple as well as unique: no tangency, no fold, and the
  implicit function theorem applies to the sensitivity terms that the
  FoM bounds rely on.

[5] the two transition questions

  (i) Churchill.  core_v2.churchill is a single expression valid for
      all Re; there is no laminar branch and no switch at Re = 2300, so
      the 'transition peak' of a two-correlation code does not exist
      here.  Monotonicity in Re is proved by composition inside
      ivcore.iv_churchill, not by sampling: every factor of
          f = 8 [(8/Re)^12 + (a+b)^-1.5]^(1/12)
      is monotone in Re for fixed relative roughness.  What the model
      does have is a steep but smooth change of slope near Re ~ 3000.
      The certified flow intervals keep Re well above that:

  (ii) Yue Table 2.  K(Re_tube) is piecewise linear through five
       printed points, so it has four breakpoints and the interval
       image must not assume smoothness.  ivcore.iv_table2_total
       takes the union of the segment ends plus every printed
       breakpoint strictly inside the interval, which is exact for a
       continuous piecewise-linear function.  The test below checks
       containment against 4000 point evaluations spread over the
       interval, including the breakpoints themselves.
