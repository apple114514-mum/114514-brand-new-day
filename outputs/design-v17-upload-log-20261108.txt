==============================================================================
[C] why K_R = 1 is a requirement of the similarity law, not a free target
==============================================================================
declared: L_R=0.5, d_R=0.616 -> A_R=0.379456, but L_R^2=0.2500
          the model is geometrically DISTORTED (fat), and this has to be
          declared, not hidden: it is exactly the case that makes the
          'ideal ratio' a declaration rather than a formula.
u_R = m_R/A_R = 0.707065  vs  sqrt(L_R) = 0.707107  (-0.006%)
steady NC: u^3 = 2 g beta H Q /(K rho cp A)  =>  u_R^2 = H_R/K_R
u_R = sqrt(L_R)  <=>  K_R = H_R/L_R = 1
=> the valve must reproduce the PROTOTYPE K at the design point; 22.8 is
   therefore a consequence, not a chosen target.

[D] forward computation of the FoM on both sides
==============================================================================
D = u_M/u_M^ideal - 1 = (K_M/K_P)^(-1/3) - 1
  u_M^ideal = model velocity if K_M were exactly K_P at the same Q_M.
  eps_Q = 0 on both sides by construction, so this D is the pure loss-
  ratio term. For Zhao it equals (1.088839)^(-1/3)-1 = the -2.79% branch,
  NOT the +2.88% figure, which carries the eps_Q accounting. Do not mix.
