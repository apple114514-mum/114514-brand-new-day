[2] the identity, and what its residual actually tests
  Define R_H = (Q_net,HS,M / Q_net,HS,P) (cp_HS,P / cp_HS,M) with
  Q_net = m cp DeltaT over the first heated segment.  Then
      (1 + D_T)(1 + D_j) = R_H
  holds ALGEBRAICALLY: the two sides are the same expression, because
  D_j scales m by A_R and D_T scales DeltaT by 1/A_R.  The residual
  below is therefore a float-arithmetic check, not a physics check.
  The physical content sits in the VALUE of R_H, i.e. in how unequal
  the two heater loss fractions are.
